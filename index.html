Primera Suite

A personal suite of color grading DCTLs for primary balancing and look development — built for color-managed workflows, tested against OpenDRT, ACES 2.0, and beyond.

Primera consolidates the grading approaches I reach for most often into one suite, under one name, and with only the controls I actually use. Most of the underlying math comes from tried-and-tested publicly-available imaging science — and from extensive use of open-source DCTLs by generous members of the color community.

My personal aesthetic lodestar is a film look in the broad sense: not emulating any specific stock or process (with one exception) but chasing personal sense-memories of seeing and working with film. The tools are flexible enough to get you most anywhere else you want to go.

These are meant to be used in the context of a fully color-managed workflow. They've been used and tested most against OpenDRT, but play well with Resolve's CST, 2499 DRT, and ACES 2.0.

Primary Grading

Primera

Primera.dctl

The foundation. Provides primary grading controls for shot-to-shot balancing across a range of log formats, from LogC3 and LogC4 to S-Log3, REDLog3G10, ACEScct, DaVinci Intermediate, Kodak Cineon, and Fuji F-Log2.

  • Exposure — Linear gain in photographic stops (2n) applied before the selected transfer function
  • Black Point — Smooth compression of the darkest tones approaching black (flare)
  • Temp / Tint — White balance operating in linear space
  • Contrast — Log-space tonal stretch/squash around a variable Pivot, defaulting to the transfer function's mid-grey
  • Shadows / Highlights — Linear gain constrained below/above mid-grey; LUT-encodable (no spatial ops)
  • Roll Off — tanh highlight compression controlling shoulder shape
  • Neg. / Pos. Saturation — Multiplicative negative gain on chroma + HSV-based density boost, with Preserve Luma offset
  • Show Chart — Per-transfer-function stop-graduated step chart, à la Volpatto

Hue & Density

Primera Hue

PrimeraHue.dctl

Per-channel hue and density control via tetrahedral interpolation, based on hotgluebanjo's DCTL implementation of the approach described by Steve Yedlin ASC in his DisplayPrep demo (2018).

  • 6 Hue sliders (R/Y/G/C/B/M) — Rodrigues rotation around each corner's achromatic axis; +/−60° per channel covers the full 360°
  • 6 Density sliders — Makes the shifted color subjectively more colorful without adding energy
  • Soft / Hard Clamp — tanh shoulder + exponential toe compression, or hard clip to [0,1]; stackable
  • Cinecolor — Emulates a budget two-strip color process (~late '30s–early '50s), similar to Technicolor Process 2; bipacked ortho negatives + duplitized print stock, without the registration nightmares
  • Protect Skintones — Applies only to Cinecolor; holdout matte centered on ~28° HSV with smooth falloff

Split Toning

Primera Split

PrimeraSplit.dctl

Subtractive color split-toning for imbuing shadows and highlights with (usually) contrasting color casts. Lately conceptualized less as an effect and more as a fundamental look development tool — defining the chroma dimensions of the characteristic curve.

  • Mid-Grey Pivot — Defaults to the selected transfer function's mid-grey; set by eye
  • Transition Softness — Controls the blend width between shadow and highlight zones
  • Preserve Luminance — As in Primera and PrimeraHue
  • Ramp Position — Positions the greyscale ramp vertically; useful when blanking has been applied
  • Show Curve / Show Pivot / Show Chart — Diagnostic overlays for curve shape, pivot position, and per-TF stop chart

Skintone Sculpting

Primera Skin

PrimeraSkin.dctl

A dedicated skintone sculpting tool operating in HSV, meant for log-based timelines. Targets a "fuzzy pie slice" of the HSV disc centered on the nominal skin tone hue angle (~28°) and applies adjustments within that region only. Everything outside the mask passes through untouched. Gamut containment via the same tanh / exponential soft squeeze used throughout the suite.

  • Hue — Rotates skin hue (+/−20°)
  • Saturation — Scales HSV saturation within the mask
  • Density — Adjusts value/brightness (positive = darker)
  • Range — Widens or narrows the skin mask (0.25 = tight, 2.0 = broad)
  • Evenness — Compresses hues toward the skin center; emulates HMU evening-out talent skintones on set
  • Low / High Gate — Value-based gates excluding dark or bright pixels from the mask
  • Show Mask — Three-zone false-color overlay: gold = in zone, red = too clockwise, cyan = too counter-clockwise

Notes

  • The math underlying the Primera tools is available in both Typst source and PDF. Feedback welcome.
  • These tools are designed for color-managed workflows. See here for a primer if you're new to the approach.
  • It is possible to produce negative or otherwise out-of-range values despite the guardrails in place. Reach for gamut compression first. If something's really broken, please file an issue.
  • Kaur Hendriksen's free standalone DCTL implementing ACES 2.0 gamut compression coefficients is warmly recommended. Resolve's ACES Transform or CST also work.
